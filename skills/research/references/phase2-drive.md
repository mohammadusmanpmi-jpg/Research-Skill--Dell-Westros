## 2.1 Discover – desire and sophistication

**S1 Life Force 8 audit** – for each of the 8 desires (definitions.md): means-end chain from product to desire; score 1-5 on emotional charge, white space (vs `01-ads.md`), product credibility; cite data.

**S2 Stated vs real desire** – "They say: … / They mean: … / Evidence: quote – N× across M sources."

**S3 Desire compass** – one sentence, checked against counts.

**S4 Default desire trap** – run the 5 checks in definitions.md. Any "yes" → go back and name the underserved desire.

**S5 Sophistication stage** (1-5, definitions.md) – cite competitor ads and customer beliefs. Implications for hook style, body copy, proof architecture, landing page.

## 2.2 Discover – objections, avatar, awareness

**S6 Objection map** – 7-10 rows: exact language | band | real meaning | Trust / Identity / Risk / Cost | preempt / reframe / prove / demonstrate | funnel stage.

**S7 Psychographic avatar** – primary: identity statement, belief system, self-narrative, stated desire, real desire, pressure point, each with quotes and counts. A "litmus test" paragraph the person would read and say "that's me." Secondary segments only where ≥50 quotes support them (use the `segment` counts).

**S8 Awareness level** (definitions.md) – where the majority sits, with evidence; implications for funnel, hook, copy length, proof density, landing page.

## 2.3 Reframe

**S9 Category gap analysis** – table: what the category SAYS (ads) | what customers EXPERIENCE (VOC) | the gap. If `01-ads.md` was not collected, build the "says" column from the website extraction and competitor product pages and flag it as weaker.

**S10 Reframe candidates** – per gap: category norm, contradicting data, reframe sentence, why it works, risk. Rank by emotional charge × white space × provability.

**S11 Four-line reframe chains** – top 3: VALIDATE (their words) / CRACK (a specific fact or question) / REFRAME / BRIDGE (to the mechanism). Each line cites data.

**S12 Skepticism pre-emption** – per blocking belief: what is underneath, strategy, the data point that informs it.

## 2.4 Ignite

**S13 Emotional driver matrix** – per segment: primary emotion, secondary, peak moment (specific scene/trigger), best ad format for it.

**S14 Proof architecture** – rank the 7 proof types (definitions.md) for this audience using the Trust Map: strength 1-5, objection it beats, asset/footage needed.

**S15 Conversion amplifiers** – true urgency only: compounding exposure, finite window, growing cost of inaction (cite failed-solution costs). No fake scarcity.

## 2.5 Variate and Evolve

**S16 Angle generation** – 8-15 angles: name | type (problem / solution / mechanism / identity / social proof / contrarian / story) | one-sentence statement | data citation | segment | scores 1-5 for freshness, emotional charge, provability, format fit.

**S18 Segment × pain matrix** – rows: top 10 pains; columns: segments; cells 1-5 from the segment-split counts in `01-counts.md`.

**S19 Test order** – Batch 1 (weeks 1-2), Batch 2 (3-4), Batch 3 (5-6), Hold (needs verification: what).

**S20 Creative DNA template** – empty fields: winning hook, winning scene, winning angle, failed concept + why, source provenance. Phase 3 and later test results fill these.
