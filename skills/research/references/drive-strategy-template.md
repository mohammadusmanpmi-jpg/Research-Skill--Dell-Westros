# Phase 4 – The DRIVE Strategy document → `04-drive-strategy.md`

The final deliverable. It follows the layout and terms of the client-approved DRIVE Strategy (the Element Vital Oct 21 document): an executive summary, then client-side fixes, then the five DRIVE parts and the appendices. It is written from the phase files; nothing new is researched here.

**Inputs:**
- `00-brief.md`, `00-account.md`, `00-business.md`, `00-offer-audit.md`
- `02-consolidation.md`, `02-drive.md`
- `01-science.md`, `01-ad-matrix.md`, `01-trends.md`, `01-tiktok.md`
- `03-angles.md`

**Terms (use exactly these):**
- **ICP** – each buyer type, with the 8 ICP fields below. Don't present "avatar" or "persona" as a separate layer unless the user asks. Where the phase files say "avatar" or "segment", it becomes an ICP here.
- **Problem mechanism** – the true cause.
- **Solution mechanism** – why this product is the answer, in layers.
- **Big Idea** – the one concept everything orbits.
- **LF8** – the Life Force 8 drivers.

**Evidence rules we keep (beyond the original):**
- Every verbatim quote in the document links to its source post or review.
- Every competitor count (e.g. "0 of 46 ads") cites its logged search in `01-ad-searches.md`.
- Every number about the client (review counts, customers) shows the live-checked figure from `00-account.md`.
- Every science line cites `01-science.md`.

**Length:** as long as it needs to be. The reference document is 53 pages. Publish it as a doc when the user wants a shareable version.

## Executive Summary
1. **What this is:** the operating system for every ad, angle, test and batch for the next six months. State the evidence base in one sentence: N verbatim excerpts from N platforms, N competitor ads, the client's ad history, N academic sources.
2. **The strategic situation in one paragraph:** from `00-business.md`.
3. **The Big Idea this document orbits:** boxed, 4-6 sentences.
4. **This positioning does five things at once:** five bullets. It explains without blame, it's checkable, it's true (traces to research), it claims ground competitors can't, and it opens every door (each ICP).
5. **What this document delivers:** one line per DRIVE part.

## Before any of this runs: N fixes on the client's side
From `00-offer-audit.md`. A numbered list, each item with what to change and why. Then a short **Claim posture** paragraph: how account health (`00-account.md`) limits claim strength.

## PART ONE – DISCOVER: Find the desire. Map the market. Write the Big Idea.

**1. The Mass Desire**
- What the category sells, and what the market is actually buying.
- A boxed one-sentence **MASS DESIRE**.
- An **In their words** table: Verbatim | Who and where (with the link).

**2. The Sophistication Map**
- A table of stages 2-5: Stage | Who's at this stage | What kills them | What converts them.
- **The strategic implication:** which stage competitors run, and which stage the client's proven ads run (from `00-account.md`). Where is the open ground?

**3. The Dominant LF8 Triggers**
- A table: LF8 driver | Role (PRIMARY / SECONDARY / UNDERSERVED) | How it shows up, including how many competitor ads lead with it (`01-ad-matrix.md`, with the search reference).
- A **STRATEGIC NOTE** naming the unowned doorways.

**4. ICP Map: Three Obvious, Three Underserved**
Each ICP card uses exactly these fields:

```
ICP n: <Name> (<age range>)          [add "(UNDERSERVED)" for 4-6]
  Trigger event: … + one verbatim quote (linked)
  Mental state: …
  What he/she's tried: …
  Awareness stage: …
  Sophistication stage: …
  Dominant LF8: …
  Belief blocking the sale: …
  Emotional doorway: …
  Note / Caution: (proven ad on account, medication interactions, safety) – optional
  WHY THIS IS UNDERSERVED: (ICPs 4-6 only) competitor-ad counts + VOC counts + client proof
```

- **Parked segments:** a table of Segment | Why it waits. Include segments with real VOC weight that aren't launched yet, each with its reason. A segment with the largest VOC count and no competitors is never silently parked. Recommend a controlled test lane instead.

**5. The Big Idea**
- Boxed.
- **Why this Big Idea works:** 5-6 bullets.
- **Test for the Big Idea:** a read-aloud test, plus any market evidence such as a proven client ad.

**DISCOVER Output Summary:** a table with Mass desire | Sophistication map | Dominant LF8 | Big Idea | ICPs.

## PART TWO – REFRAME: Break the belief blocking the sale.

**Part 1: The Problem Mechanism**
- **The true cause:** plain-word bullets at grade-6 level, each with a citation (`01-science.md` § 1), ending in a boxed one-line true cause.
- **A note on honesty:** where the evidence limits the claim (e.g. "for most men, not low T", never "never your hormones").
- **Why Other Products Fail:** a table of What they tried | Why they bought it | Why it failed (the reason, then their words, linked).
- **The category gap:** what every brand says, and the 2-3 things nobody has given the buyer.

**Part 2: The Solution Mechanism**
- 2-4 layers. Each layer has a **THE CLAIM** / **THE WHY** / **THE PROOF** block, kept inside the ingredient evidence ceiling (`01-science.md` § 2).
- Boxed: the layers stacked into **ONE LINE HE REMEMBERS**.
- **Mechanism distinctness:** every mechanism line has passed the check in `phase3-angles.md` Step 4b, so no line nearly copies a competitor's mechanism phrase.

**The Four-Line Reframe Chain:** a table of Current belief | True cause | Why others fail | Why this is different. Every angle must be writable as this chain.

**Visual Proof Architecture:** 4-6 hero visuals. Each has a name, a description with its on-screen caption and source, **Why it works**, and any rule or dependency (e.g. "real customer logs only", "only after fix #1").

**Skepticism Architecture: Pre-empting Every Objection:** a table of The objection | How the reframe pre-empts it. Include the medication / safety question when `01-science.md` § 3 flags interactions.

**REFRAME Output Summary:** the four-line master reframe and the list of visual proof beats.

## PART THREE – IGNITE: Load the emotional fuel. Define the persuasion sequence.

**Layer 1: Emotional Drivers Per ICP**
- A table: ICP | Primary driver | Secondary | How it shows up | Tone.
- A **KEY INSIGHT** box: the emotional lane competitors aren't using.

**Layer 2: Conversion Amplifiers**
- **PROOF:** a proof hierarchy for this product (in order) and the persona rule ("real credentialed people only, or none").
- **URGENCY:** true urgency only, such as a biological clock, a seasonal window, or the test clock. No fake countdowns.
- **RISK REMOVAL:** the category guarantee standard against the client's, then two guarantee frames to test (e.g. a confidence frame and a log frame).
- **OFFER ARCHITECTURE:** a table of Offer | Structure | Best for | Frame, built on the **real buy box** from `00-offer-audit.md` (real prices, price per day). Then a bonus stack to test.

**Layer 3: The Persuasion Stack**
- A 5-position table: # | Position | What happens | Client example. The positions are 1 Ego activation, 2 Identity tension, 3 Mechanism, 4 Proof stack, 5 Offer + identity CTA.
- **Length-specific adaptations:** short-form video 30-45 s with timings, long-form native static (400-1,500 words), and UGC talking head (45-90 s).

**The Humour Principle in This Category:** where it works, and where it doesn't.

**IGNITE Output Summary.**

## PART FOUR – VARIATE: Build the angles. Choose the formats. Feed the algorithm.

- **THE STRUCTURE** box: 1 Big Idea > N angles > 2-3 formats per angle = N concepts. Batch size is set by account health.
- **The Angle Library.** About 12 angle cards in the angle card format (`phase3-angles.md` Step 5), each a different emotional doorway into the same Big Idea:

```
ANGLE nn: <Name>
 ICP TARGET        …
 SOPHISTICATION    Stage n. <lead type>
 PRIMARY DRIVER    …
 FORMATS           2-3 formats
 BASIS             client proven ad (days) / VOC quotes (linked) / science (cited) / competitor-ad count (search ref) / trend (01-trends.md)
 VALIDATION FLAG   OPEN / EMERGING / PROVEN / CROWDED / ABANDONED / PROVEN ON ACCOUNT (+ evidence line)
HOOK OPTIONS       3 hooks
MECHANISM ANCHOR   1-3 sentences (passed the distinctness check)
PROOF BEATS REQUIRED  3-4 bullets
CTA FRAME          1-2 lines
PRODUCTION NOTES   casting, copy traps, holds ("HOLD until …"), test-lane notes, what to fix on the live version
```

- **Format Assignment Matrix:** a table of Format | When to use it, what it costs, what it delivers (and which angles).
- **Production Protection: The Non-Negotiables:** a table of Non-negotiable | What it is | What dies if it's lost. Typically the scroll-stop, the signal/mechanism reveal, the proof moment, and the offer line.
- **Recommended Launch Batch (First 4 Weeks):** a table of # | Angle | Format(s) | ICP | Priority (PROVEN (iterate) / PROVEN CHASSIS / HIGH PRIORITY (UNDERSERVED) / WHITE SPACE / CHEAP VOLUME / VOLUME TEST / DIVERSITY TEST / CONTROLLED TEST / HOLD). End with a **LAUNCH BATCH SUMMARY** box.
- **Testing budget:** 60% on proven angles, 30% on high-priority new angles, 10% on frontier tests (name them).

## PART FIVE – EVOLVE: Read the data. Extract patterns. Compound the advantage.

- **The Diagnostic Framework:** a table of Metric failure | Diagnosis | Where to fix it. Rows: hook rate <20%, hold <30%, CTR <1%, high CTR with low add to cart, CVR <2%, high refund rate, ad rejected / account warning, low repeat purchase. See course-playbook.md § 7.
- **Pattern Extraction Questions (After Each Cycle):** 8-10 questions specific to this brand's lanes, ICPs, proof beats, drivers, formats, hook types, CTA frames and test lanes.
- **The Four-Week Production Workflow:** Week 1 Strategy, Week 2 Briefs, Week 3 Production, Week 4 Launch + Learn.
- **Creative Velocity Targets:** concepts per launch, refresh cycle, hook variants per winner, formats per winning angle, budget split.

## APPENDICES
- **A. Headline Library by Doorway.** 4-5 headlines per doorway: signal/fear, identity, curiosity, demonstration, care/partner, plus the product's own lanes.
- **B. Beat Maps.** 3 beat maps (short-form B-roll + VO, long-form native static, UGC talking head), each tied to a named angle and ICP. These are beat maps, not scripts.
- **C. Claim Guardrails (Safest Words and Copy Traps).** The three-column table and the copy traps from `01-science.md` § 4.
- **D. The Scientific Footing.** The problem facts with full citations, the ingredient evidence-ceiling table, and **What we don't claim, and why** (`01-science.md`).
- **E. The Metrics Dashboard.** A table of Metric | Target | What it tells us (hook, hold, CTR, CVR, AOV against the real bundle prices, ROAS, refund rate, repeat order by day 90, ad rejections).
- **F. Competitive Watch.** A table of Competitor type | Examples (with revenue / ad-count estimates) | Our strategic response.
- **G. Trend Watch** (our addition). UNRIDDEN and EARLY RIDERS trends from `01-trends.md`, with their act-by window and the angle each feeds.
- **H. Evidence Index** (our addition). Counts per platform from `01-depth.md`, the verification result from `01-verification.md`, the search-log reference for every competitor count, and the link to the quote file.

## The Strategic Mandate
Close with one page: what happens if the fixes land first, the library runs at the volume the account can carry, every cycle feeds the next, and the underserved ICPs get the creative nobody else is making. End with the win condition.

4. No mechanism line or hook nearly copies a competitor (Step 4b check logged).
